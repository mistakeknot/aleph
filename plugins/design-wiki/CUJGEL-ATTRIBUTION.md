The bundled cujgel.schema.json is a validation-only snapshot of the MIT-licensed
Cujgel schema at commit ca692c86136f9a5579b9780725e5a2ecd9e7802d:
https://github.com/mistakeknot/cujgel/blob/ca692c86136f9a5579b9780725e5a2ecd9e7802d/spec/cujgel.schema.json
